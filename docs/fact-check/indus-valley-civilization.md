## Title, description, image

Meta description says the Harappans had "flush toilets" and "vanished without a trace". No source shows flush toilets, and the people did not vanish. Suggested fix: "Over 4,000 years ago, a civilization built planned cities with covered drains, then faded away. Nobody can read its writing."
