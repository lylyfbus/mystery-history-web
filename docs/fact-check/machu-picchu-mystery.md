## Title, description, image

Description says "stumbled upon Machu Picchu" and "500-year-old" and implies Bingham found it; a local farmer led him there and the site is about 600 years old by the 2021 dating. Suggested fix: "Hiram Bingham was led to Machu Picchu in 1911 - a citadel of the Inca, more than 500 years old."
