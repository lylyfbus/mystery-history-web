## Title, description, image

The title calls it "Boston's Deadliest Syrup Disaster", which is an odd claim that is not checkable. Suggested fix: "The Great Molasses Flood: When Boston Was Hit by a Wave of Syrup". Meta description and hero alt text are fine.
