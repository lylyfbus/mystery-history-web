# Fact check: wow-signal

Checked: 2026-11-15

| Claim in the story | Result | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The signal was recorded at 11:16 PM EDT on August 15, 1977 | Correct | https://www.bigear.org/Wow30th/wow30th.htm |
| Big Ear at Ohio State University picked it up | Correct | https://www.bigear.org/wow.htm |
