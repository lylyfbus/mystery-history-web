## Title, description, image

Title says El Dorado "Drove Conquistadors Mad". This is a figure of speech, and the description says El Dorado "was not a city but a person"; sources show the legend grew from a ritual story, and historians disagree about whether the ritual is real. Suggested fix: description "El Dorado began as a story of a Muisca ruler covered in gold dust. ...". Image alt text was not checked. Book: "El Dorado, land of gold" by Dennis Abrams could not be checked.
