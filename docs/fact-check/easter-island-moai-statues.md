## Title, description, image

Title says "How Did a Lost Civilization Move 1,000 Giant Stone Statues?" and the description says "Nearly 1,000 massive stone statues" and giants "weighing 80 tons". Sources say about 900 statues, the Rapa Nui people were never lost (about 7,750 live there), and the typical moai weighs about 12.5 tons (the heaviest raised is about 86 tons). Suggested fix: "How Did the Rapa Nui Move About 900 Giant Stone Statues?" and "...how did they move giants that weighed up to 80 tons?". Image alt text was not checked.
