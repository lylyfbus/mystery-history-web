## Title, description, image

Title says "8,000 Unique Warriors" and the description says "Each has a unique face": faces are made from a few molds and then changed by hand, so "unique" is too strong. The description also says "the emperor they guard has never been found": his tomb mound is known and unopened, so "never been opened" is correct.
