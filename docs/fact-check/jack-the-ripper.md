## Title, description, image

Meta description says he "taunted police with letters" and "murdered five women" as fact. Most experts think the letters were written by a journalist, and the link between the five murders is an opinion. Suggested fix: "In 1888, five women were killed in Whitechapel, London. The killer was never caught. More than a century later, his identity is still unknown."
