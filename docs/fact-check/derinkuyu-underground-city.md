## Title, description, image

Title says "20,000 People Lived Beneath Turkey!" and the description says it "housed 20,000 people across 18 levels". Sources say "as many as 20,000" and level counts differ (8, 11, 18). Suggested fix: "Up to 20,000 People May Have Sheltered Beneath Turkey" and "...a vast underground city that may have sheltered up to 20,000 people". The image alt text was not checked.
