# Claims to check

Claims in the posts that look doubtful. They were NOT changed. The owner decides.

| Post | Claim in the text | Why it is doubtful |
|---|---|---|
| jack-the-ripper | Dr. Thomas Bond "examined Eddowes and Kelly" | Records say Bond examined Kelly. Dr. Frederick Gordon Brown examined Eddowes; Bond only read the notes. The rewrite says "examined Kelly and studied the case of Eddowes". |
| jack-the-ripper | Patricia Cornwell spent "an estimated $2 million" | Later reports give higher numbers (up to about $6 million). |
| jack-the-ripper | "An estimated 900,000 people" lived in poverty in the East End | 900,000 is the usual number for the whole East End population, not only the poor. The rewrite says "About 900,000 people lived in the East End, and many had almost nothing". |
